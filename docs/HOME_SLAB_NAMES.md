# Homepage slab trade names

Research date: 2026-10-17. Labels: `components/cinematic/slabs.ts`.

All 13 cards and enlarged views use existing commercial stone names as their
headings in Hebrew, Arabic, English and Russian. The Latin trade name is also
visible in translated views. The former descriptive names have been removed.

## Evidence and limits

Research used the original photos in `catalog/original-stone/`, Google Lens reverse
image results, and direct visual comparisons with supplier product photos. The
edited homepage images contain reconstructed areas and were not the primary
identification evidence. The earliest repository PDF (17a292f) also has generic
slab captions. The inventory sticker in source 04 is too blurred to read reliably.

These are **tentative visual identifications**, not verified inventory records.
The localized note under the collection explains this once. Cards and enlarged
views show only the trade names and slab number; the repeated identification
sentence was removed from every image caption at the owner's request.
References establish the trade names and comparison images; they do not establish
who supplied these particular slabs or their SKU, quarry, origin, composition,
performance or availability. Do not infer those properties from the headings.
Supplier confirmation can resolve remaining identity uncertainty.
