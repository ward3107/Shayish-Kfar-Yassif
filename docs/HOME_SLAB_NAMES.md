# Homepage slab labels

Research date: 2026-10-17. Labels are maintained in `components/cinematic/slabs.ts`.

The 13 source images have numeric filenames and no supplier/product identifiers.
The displayed images are retouched front-view visualizations, including reconstructed
areas. A similar photograph in a stone catalog cannot establish the actual material,
supplier, SKU, country of origin, or commercial name of these particular slabs.

Every slab therefore has a localized **descriptive display name**, not an invented
inventory name. Six also show a clearly qualified **possible visual match**. The
qualification appears on the card and in its enlarged view, in all four languages.
Replace a possible match with a verified trade name only after receiving supplier
documentation for the corresponding slab ID.
